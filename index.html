Capstone Project

IoT-Powered Attendance Monitoring with Geotagging and Geolocation to Address Student Truancy

Role: One of the Developers

Our capstone project is about developing an IoT-powered attendance monitoring system that uses RFID, GPS, geotagging, and geolocation to address student truancy. The system allows students to scan their RFID IDs when entering or leaving the school and verifies their location to make sure they are within the authorized school premises. Parents receive SMS notifications about their children's entry and exit, while teachers and administrators can monitor attendance through a web-based dashboard.

The main goal is to improve attendance accuracy, prevent proxy attendance and unauthorized exits, and help parents become more aware of their children's school attendance and safety.

View Project
How the system works

Step 1: RFID Scanning

The student scans their RFID ID when entering or leaving through the school gate.

Step 2: GPS and Geolocation

The IoT device collects GPS coordinates and checks whether the student is within the authorized school area.

Step 3: Attendance Validation

The system validates the ID, location, and time before recording the attendance.

Step 4: Parent Notification

Parents receive an SMS notification when their child enters or leaves the school.
